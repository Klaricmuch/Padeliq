Questions

Everything you're wondering.

Can I record with my phone?+

Yes. A phone on a tripod or clipped to the fence is exactly what we designed for: one fixed camera, one angle.

How long does analysis take?+

Most clips come back in a few minutes. You upload, we track, you get your breakdown.

Does it work indoors?+

Yes, indoor and outdoor courts both work, as long as the four court corners are visible in the frame.

Can I upload doubles?+

Absolutely. The demo you see is a real doubles rally tracked end to end.

Do I need sensors or wearables?+

No. Everything comes from the video itself, with no watches, no ball sensors, no extra gear.

How accurate is it?+

Ball speed and bounces are measured from the tracked ball on a calibrated court, and low-confidence shots are flagged rather than guessed.
